Who We Are

A movement rooted in radical hope

Sarthak Foundation was born from a simple conviction: that every person on this planet deserves clean air, clean water, and a living ecosystem to call home.

Founded in 2012 by a coalition of ecologists, community leaders, and everyday citizens, we have grown into a global force for environmental restoration — without losing the grassroots soul that drives us.

"The environment is not a cause. It's the ground beneath everything else we care about."

Our model is simple: local action, global network. Change happens community by community, tree by tree, river by river.

Our Impact

Measured in ecosystems restored

Numbers tell part of the story. The rest is in the sound of a river running clear again, the return of bird species not seen for decades, and the faces of children inheriting a world worth inheriting.

In 2024 alone, our projects sequestered 1.8 million tonnes of CO₂, provided clean water to 400,000 people, and restored habitat for 140 threatened species.
